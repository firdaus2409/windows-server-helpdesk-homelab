# Locked out or forgot your password

**Audience:** All staff (external knowledge base, shown in the help portal)

## If Windows says your account is locked

Your account locks after 5 wrong password attempts. It unlocks by itself after 15 minutes.

1. Wait 15 minutes.
2. Check that Caps Lock is off, then try again slowly.
3. Still locked, or need to log in urgently? Raise a "Get IT help" request on the help portal.

## If you've forgotten your password

1. Raise a "Get IT help" request on the help portal.
2. IT will confirm your identity, then give you a temporary password separately (never by email or in the ticket).
3. Log in with the temporary password. Windows will ask you to choose a new one.

## Password rules

- At least 10 characters.
- Use at least 3 of these: uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, symbols.
- Don't include your username or name.

## Stay safe

IT will never ask for your password. If an email asks you to "verify" your password, don't click the link. Forward it to IT.
