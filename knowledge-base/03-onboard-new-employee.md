# Onboard a new employee

**Audience:** IT support (internal knowledge base)

**Trigger:** HR request with the person's name, department, job title and start date

## Steps

1. In ADUC, open HomeLab > Staff > [department OU].
2. Right-click a colleague in the same role > Copy. Copying carries over their group memberships.
3. Enter first name, last name and a logon name in the format firstname.lastname.
4. Enter a temporary password and tick "User must change password at next logon". Click Finish.
5. Open the new user's Properties:
   - Member Of: confirm the correct department group (SG-IT, SG-Finance or SG-HR).
   - Organization: set Job Title and Department.
6. Give the temporary password to the new starter separately on their first day.

## Verify

First login prompts for a password change, and the department drive appears (F: for Finance).
